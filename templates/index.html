01 / About

A practical
engineering mindset.

My route into cybersecurity started with diagnosing real physical systems under operational constraints. Abdulrazaq Adeyemi is also sometimes written as Abdulrasaq Adeyemi.

As an Electrical Maintenance Technician in industrial facilities in Nigeria, I inspected equipment, measured motor current, investigated overloads and thermic relay trips, read electrical schematics, and traced faults to their root causes. That experience developed habits that transfer naturally to security work: structured troubleshooting, safety awareness, technical documentation, and disciplined investigation.

I am currently studying for a B.Sc. in Cybersecurity at the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB), with expected graduation in 2028. My academic interests include network security, applied cryptography, operating system security, information assurance, and database security.

My professional interests are SOC analysis, threat detection and incident response, vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, cloud security, application security, security automation, and DevSecOps. These are areas of development, not claims of seniority.
