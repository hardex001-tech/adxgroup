01 / About the person behind ADX

Not just collecting
certificates.

I came to cybersecurity through hands-on engineering, not a single moment of inspiration. Today I study security weaknesses across public infrastructure, cloud environments, and software systems.

As an Electrical Maintenance Technician in industrial facilities in Nigeria, I diagnosed motor overload alarms and thermic relay trips, read schematics, traced faults, and isolated root causes while operations were under pressure. That work taught me to observe, isolate, test, and verify before deciding what to change.

I now apply the same discipline to networks, logs, security events, and controlled labs while studying for a B.Sc. in Cybersecurity at the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB), expected in 2028. My academic focus includes network security, applied cryptography, operating systems security, information assurance, and database security.

I am developing toward SOC Analyst, junior penetration testing, DevSecOps, and cloud security automation roles. Certificates support the learning; repositories, lab notes, testing, and remediation show how I apply it.
