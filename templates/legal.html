Enterprise Privacy Policy & Data Protection

At ADX Group (operating via adxgroup.tech), we maintain a strict Zero-Data-Leak mandate. This Privacy Policy details our handling of organizational identifiers, telemetry queries, and authentication records in strict accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA / NDPR), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the European Digital Services Act (DSA).

1. Data Controller Identity & Contact

The Data Controller responsible for perimeter telemetry and identity verification processing is ADX Group Cyber Systems, under the direction of Lead Cyber Systems Architect Abdulrazaq Adeyemi. For data subject requests, right-to-be-forgotten petitions, or compliance inquiries, contact our dedicated security desk at contact@adxgroup.tech.

2. Zero-Retention Diagnostic Queries

When an organization or authorized user submits a domain for infrastructure diagnostic via our 6-Gate Audit Engine, ADX processes public Internet routing records, SSL/TLS handshake certificates, SPF/DMARC records, and HTTP response headers. We do not inspect, intercept, store, or modify private payload contents. Diagnostic records are ephemeral and bound solely to your authenticated workspace.

3. Zero-Data-Leak Autonomous AI Systems

All enterprise artificial intelligence queries executed through the ADX Intelligence division utilize dedicated private memory structures. Customer proprietary code, architectural secrets, and compliance records are never exposed to public LLM training datasets or multi-tenant pipelines.

4. User Rights Under NDPA & GDPR

Every user is entitled to full sovereign control over their records, including the right of access, rectification, data portability, and unconditional account deletion. Users can permanently purge their account and all associated telemetry records directly within the ADX Console at any time.
