# ADX Group Brand Identity Specification

## 1. Brand Concept & Crest
The ADX Group identity is defined by the precision **Hexagonal Cyber Shield** containing the interlocking stylized **'A' and 'X'** structural vector marks. It embodies our foundational philosophy: **"Silent Innovation. Absolute Defense."**

## 2. Brand Asset Roster
* `original/logo.svg`: Unmodified vector master archive (256x256).
* `logo-mark-flat.svg`: Single-color flat mark using `currentColor`. Built with thick stroke weights for clarity down to 16px.
* `logo-lockup.svg`: Flat mark + "ADX GROUP" wordmark in Inter 700 with `+0.02em` tracking and text converted to vector outlines.
* `logo-lockup-tagline.svg`: Lockup with the official cyan tagline *"Silent Innovation. Absolute Defense."* for hero, footer, and desktop headers &gt; 1024px.
* `logo-mark-gradient.svg`: High-resolution holographic gradient version for large hero banners and social media.
* `favicon.svg` & PNG Suite (`favicon-32.png`, `favicon-192.png`, `favicon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`): 1:1 square assets on `#0B1220` navy background.
* `og-image.png`: 1200x630 Open Graph / Twitter social preview graphic.

## 3. Minimum Sizing
* **Standalone Mark (`logo-mark-flat.svg`):** Minimum **16px** height/width.
* **Horizontal Lockup (`logo-lockup.svg`):** Minimum **120px** width.
* **Lockup with Tagline (`logo-lockup-tagline.svg`):** Minimum **180px** width.

## 4. Clear Space
* Always maintain clear breathing room around the logo equal to at least the thickness of the **'X'** blade arm (approx. 25% of the mark height) on all four sides.

## 5. Color Rules & WCAG AA Contrast
* **On Dark Navy (`#0B1220`, `#111A2E`):**
  - Primary mark / text: `#E6EAF2` (Contrast 14.8:1 &mdash; AAA)
  - Accent / Tagline: `#38bdf8` (Contrast 8.76:1 &mdash; AAA) or `#2DD4BF` (Contrast 10.7:1 &mdash; AAA)
* **On Light / White:**
  - Mark / text: `#0B1220` (Contrast 17.5:1 &mdash; AAA)
* **Status Colors:**
  - Success: `#10B981` | Warning: `#F59E0B` | Danger: `#EF4444`

## 6. Do's & Don'ts
* **DO:** Use `currentColor` flat lockup for headers, footers, auth forms, and dashboard navigation.
* **DO:** Use absolute URLs for Open Graph metadata (`https://adxgroup.tech/assets/brand/og-image.png`).
* **DON'T:** Apply drop shadows or neon glow filters to the mark below 48px.
* **DON'T:** Use negative tracking or crowd the letterforms of "ADX GROUP".
* **DON'T:** Distort the aspect ratio or separate the 'A' and 'X' blades.
